The Ferro Area Drivers Association disputed the council's traffic statistics at its annual meeting on Tuesday.

The congestion charge did not reduce downtown traffic.

The association said its own counts, taken from fuel sales data, showed no meaningful change, and that deliveries have simply shifted to the ring road rather than stopped.

The congestion charge costs drivers $12 per day.

It disputed the council's $9 figure, arguing that the published rate excludes the administrative fee added at renewal.

The association has asked the council to publish the raw sensor data, and said it would commission its own study if that request was refused.
