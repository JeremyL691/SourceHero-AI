The Ferro City Gazette reviewed twelve months of traffic data after the congestion charge took effect in January 2025.

Ferro City's congestion charge reduced downtown traffic by 18% in its first year.

The council's own modelling had predicted a reduction of between 12% and 15%, so the outcome exceeded expectations.

The congestion charge costs drivers $9 per day.

Charges apply on weekdays between 7am and 7pm, and vehicles entering the charging zone are billed by number of crossings.

Retail groups reported mixed effects. Footfall in the covered streets rose slightly, while businesses near the ring road reported a visible loss of passing trade.

Two larger operators have since asked the council to review the charging hours.
