Meridian Equity Research published a note on Halcyon Cells after reviewing the company's guidance and a supply-chain survey of ceramic separator producers.

First-year HX-2 pack prices will be above $150/kWh.

That is 25% above the $120/kWh Halcyon has guided to, and it reflects separator yields that we believe are still in the low tens of percent.

The HX-2 energy density of 450 Wh/kg would lead the industry.

We accept the company's cell-level figure for planning purposes, while noting that independent testing has not yet reproduced it at that level.

Our model assumes Halcyon reaches roughly 3,000 MWh of HX-2 output in its first full year, which is below the 6,000 MWh implied by management guidance.

We rate the shares neutral and would revisit that view after the first production yields are published.
