The transport committee published its mid-year review of the congestion charge, covering the period from January to December 2025.

Downtown traffic fell by 18% after the congestion charge was introduced.

Committee members noted that the figure comes from automatic number-plate counts at four boundary sensors, and that two sensors were recalibrated in November.

The charge remains revenue-neutral after operating costs, with the surplus directed to bus frequency improvements.

Members asked for a further review once a full year of post-recalibration data is available.

=== version: 2026-07-01 ===

The transport committee published a revised mid-year review, incorporating corrected sensor readings for November and December 2025.

Revised data shows downtown traffic fell by 11% after the congestion charge was introduced.

The original 18% figure counted two vehicles more than once at a recalibrated sensor; the corrected series is the one the committee now relies on.

The charge remains revenue-neutral after operating costs, and the committee recommends keeping the current charging hours.

Members said a further review would be scheduled once twelve months of corrected data are available.
