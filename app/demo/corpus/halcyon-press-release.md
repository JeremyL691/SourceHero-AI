Halcyon Cells today provided an update on the HX-2 programme ahead of its investor briefing.

Mass production of the HX-2 will begin in September 2026.

The company reaffirmed the schedule first announced in March and said tooling at the Nevada line has been installed.

The HX-2 retained 92% of its capacity after 1,000 charge cycles.

Halcyon said this figure comes from its own validation fleet and that independent verification is expected later in the year.

Halcyon Cells expects HX-2 pack prices of $120/kWh at launch.

The company said pack pricing would decline as volumes scale, without committing to a specific curve.

=== version: 2026-08-20 ===

Halcyon Cells today provided an update on the HX-2 programme, and revised the launch schedule.

Mass production of the HX-2 has been postponed to January 2027.

The company attributed the delay to a slower-than-expected yield on the ceramic separator line, and said a second processing hall is now being installed.

The HX-2 retained 92% of its capacity after 1,000 charge cycles.

Halcyon said this figure is unchanged and comes from its own validation fleet.

Halcyon Cells expects HX-2 pack prices of $120/kWh at launch.

The company said pack pricing would decline as volumes scale, though the later launch reduces 2026 revenue expectations.
