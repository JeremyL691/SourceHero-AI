Orchid AI published Orchid-7 today under a licence that permits commercial use, redistribution and modification, including for models trained on its outputs.

Orchid-7 has 7 billion parameters.

The model uses a mixture-of-experts design with 1.2 billion active parameters per token, which the company says lets it run on a single consumer accelerator.

Orchid-7 was trained on 2 trillion tokens.

The training mix was roughly 60% English, 15% Chinese, and the remainder split across eleven other languages.

Orchid-7 scored 68% on the ReasonBench evaluation.

Orchid said it did not fine-tune on ReasonBench's test set, and published the evaluation harness alongside the weights so third parties can reproduce the figure.
